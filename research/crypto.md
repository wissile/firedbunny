## 2. How it works

**Bitcoin supply and blocks**
- Supply is capped at **21M**. Blocks arrive ~**every 10 minutes** on average. Issuance halves over time, then stops. https://bitcoin.org/en/faq
- Circulating ≈ 20.1M (≈95.7% mined, computed). The final coins are expected ~2140: UNVERIFIED (common estimate, not on bitcoin.org FAQ).
- Halvings happen every **210,000 blocks**. https://bitref.com/halving/

| Halving | Date | Block | Reward (BTC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2012-11-28 | 210,000 | 50 → 25 |
| 2 | 2016-07-09 | 420,000 | 25 → 12.5 |
| 3 | 2020-05-11 | 630,000 | 12.5 → 6.25 |
| 4 | **2024-04-20** | 840,000 | 6.25 → **3.125** |
| Next | **~Apr 2028** (est.) | **1,050,000** | → 1.5625 |

**Proof of work vs proof of stake**
- Bitcoin uses proof of work: miners compete with computing power.
- Ethereum switched to proof of stake at **The Merge, Sep 15, 2022**. Energy use fell ~**99.95%**, and validators who stake ETH now propose and validate blocks. https://ethereum.org/roadmap/merge/

**Ethereum staking**
- Solo validators need **32 ETH** (one validator can hold up to 2,048 ETH).
- Penalties: offline validators lose small amounts; provable misbehavior is "slashed" (part of the stake destroyed).
- Ways to stake: home/solo, delegated, liquid/pooled (any amount), or exchange (the highest trust assumption). https://ethereum.org/staking/
- Staking yield ≈ **2.8–3.3% APR** (Apr 2026), with a Feb 2026 network average of 2.91%. About 37M ETH (~30%+ of supply) is staked. https://blog.stake.fish/the-state-of-ethereum-in-2026/ . (ethereum.org's stale widget shows 2.5% and ~35%.)
- Staked ETH ETFs launched Oct 2025–Mar 2026; see section 3.

**Fees, smart contracts, layer 2s**
- Every transaction pays a network fee: miner fees on BTC, "gas" on ETH. Fees rise when blocks are congested.
- ethereum.org snapshot: average **$0.030 on Ethereum mainnet vs $0.0016 on L2s**. https://ethereum.org/layer-2/
- Smart contracts are programs on-chain that run automatically (lending, DEXs). Bugs in them are a major hack vector (see section 5).
- **Layer 2s / rollups** (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base-type, Starknet, ZKsync) batch transactions and post their data to Ethereum.
  - Two kinds: "optimistic" rollups and "ZK" rollups.
  - The Fusaka upgrade (**Dec 2025**) added PeerDAS for cheaper L2 data.
  - There is no "official" L2, and their safety varies. Same source.

**Stablecoins**
- **Fiat-backed** (USDT, USDC): redeemable 1:1, with reserves held by the issuer. They are centralized and depend on audits and reserves. https://ethereum.org/stablecoins/
- **Crypto-backed / synthetic** (DAI, USDS, USDe, GHO): backed by crypto collateral or hedges. Same source.
- **Algorithmic** relies on code, not reserves. Example: **TerraUSD (UST)** lost its peg in **May 2022**.
  - Investors lost an estimated ~$40B.
  - The SEC settlement is $4.47B (Jun 2024). https://www.businessinsurance.com/sec-reaches-447-billion-settlement-with-bankrupt-crypto-firm-terraform-labs/
  - Do Kwon was sentenced to **15 years** on Dec 11, 2025. https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/terraform-labs-do-kwon-gets-15-years-in-prison-in-the-us/
- US payment stablecoins now fall under the GENIUS Act (section 3): 1:1 reserves and no yield to holders.

**Keys, wallets, custody**
- Lose the private key and the coins are gone: "lost bitcoins remain dormant forever." https://bitcoin.org/en/faq
- The recovery (seed) phrase restores a wallet. Write it down and store it offline. Never share it. Same source.
- **Hot wallets** are online, convenient, and hackable. **Cold wallets** are offline (hardware or paper): more secure, but can be lost or destroyed. SEC Investor Bulletin, Dec 12, 2025. https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/crypto-asset-custody-basics-retail-investors-investor-bulletin-0
- **Exchange vs self-custody:**
  - With an exchange, the custodian holds the keys. It may rehypothecate (lend out) or commingle customer assets, and you may lose access if it is hacked or goes bankrupt.
  - With self-custody, you alone bear the security risk. Same SEC bulletin.
  - This is the meaning of "Not your keys, not your coins."

## 3. US regulation, 2025–2026

**GENIUS Act (stablecoins)**
- Signed **Jul 18, 2025** as **P.L. 119-27**. https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-genius-act-into-law/
- Rules:
  - **100% reserves** in liquid assets (USD, short-term Treasuries).
  - **Monthly** public reserve disclosure.
  - Holders get **priority in insolvency**.
  - Bank Secrecy Act / anti-money-laundering and sanctions programs apply, and issuers must be able to freeze, seize or burn coins. (White House fact sheet.)
  - **No interest or yield** to holders.
  - Banks issue only through subsidiaries.
  - Issuers with ≤$10B outstanding may opt for state supervision. https://tazapay.com/blog/genius-act-one-year-later-stablecoin-rules-2026
- **Effective date:** the earlier of 18 months after enactment (**Jan 18, 2027**) or 120 days after final rules.
- Regulators **missed** the Jul 18, 2026 rulemaking deadline: ~10 proposed rules (OCC, FDIC, NCUA, Treasury, FinCEN), none final. https://forklog.com/en/news/u-s-regulators-miss-deadline-for-implementing-genius-act

## 4. Taxes (IRS)

Main source: https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets (updated Sep 2, 2026)

**Basics**
- Digital assets are **property**, not currency. This covers crypto, stablecoins and NFTs.
- Taxable disposals: selling for USD, **swapping crypto for crypto**, and **spending** crypto. Moving coins between your own wallets is not taxable, unless you pay a fee in crypto.
- Transactions must be reported even when there is no gain.
- Gains and losses go on Form 8949. The holding period decides short- vs long-term (more than 1 year = long-term, the standard capital-asset rule). Short-term gains are taxed at ordinary rates.
- **Form 1040 digital asset question:** "During [year], did you: (a) receive (as a reward, award or payment for property or services); or (b) sell, exchange, or otherwise dispose of a digital asset?" It also appears on Forms 1041, 1065, 1120 and others.
- **Staking and mining** rewards are **ordinary income** at fair market value when you gain "dominion and control" (Schedule 1). Rev. Rul. 2023-14 (Jul 2023): https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rr-23-14.pdf
- IRS scam warning: there is **no IRS crypto "registration."** QR-code letters about a "compliance portal" are scams. Same IRS page.

**Form 1099-DA**
- Brokers report **gross proceeds for transactions on/after Jan 1, 2025** (first forms in early 2026). They report **basis for certain transactions on/after Jan 1, 2026**. IRS page above; https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1099-da
- Basis is reported only for "covered" assets acquired on/after Jan 1, 2026 in the same account (per Treasury final regulations): UNVERIFIED this session.
- De minimis thresholds of $10K for qualifying stablecoins and $600 for NFTs: UNVERIFIED.
- The **DeFi broker rule was repealed** by a Congressional Review Act resolution (H.J.Res.25) signed **Apr 10, 2025**. https://waysandmeans.house.gov/2025/04/10/president-trump-signs-ways-means-resolution-overturning-biden-administrations-burdensome-irs-defi-broker-rule

**Wash sales and pending bills**
- **Wash-sale rule (§1091) does NOT currently apply to crypto held directly.** It covers stock and securities, and no law change was enacted as of Aug 31, 2026. https://www.thetaxadviser.com/issues/2026/aug/tax-legislation-for-digital-assets-whats-the-conversation-in-congress/
- Caution for the site: aggressive loss-harvesting can still be challenged under general doctrines such as economic substance. (General caution; not an IRS crypto-specific statement.)
- Pending bills, **neither enacted**:
  - Lummis **S. 2207** (2025): wash-sale rules for digital assets; **$300 per-transaction de minimis** exclusion (up to $5,000/yr); staking income deferred until sale.
  - House **PARITY Act (H.R. 8899, May 2026)**: wash-sale rules for all digital assets; no general de minimis, but stablecoin sales near par are not taxed; staking income can be deferred up to 5 years. Same Tax Adviser source.
- **Crypto ETFs** are securities, so the **wash-sale rule applies to ETF shares** (30-day window). ETF holders also cannot claim staking income directly. https://www.financial-planning.com/news/how-spot-bitcoin-etfs-miss-out-on-major-tax-alpha

**Crypto ATM scams (FTC)**
- Losses rose ~10x from 2020 to 2023, to **>$110M in 2023**, and were **>$65M in H1 2024**.
- The median loss was **$10,000**. People 60+ lost $46M (~71%) and were >3x as likely as younger adults to report a loss.
- ~86% of reports involved government, business or tech-support impersonation. https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/new-ftc-data-shows-massive-increase-losses-bitcoin-atm-scams , https://ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/bitcoin_atms_spotlight.pdf

**Pig butchering**
- Relationship investment scams. The CFTC, SEC, FINRA and NASAA issued a joint alert.
- Advice: ignore unsolicited texts, forward spam to 7726, report to IC3.gov with wallet addresses and transaction hashes. CFTC, Sep 11, 2024. https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8963-24

**No insurance or guarantees**
- **SIPC** "does not protect any digital or crypto asset that does not qualify as a 'security.'" https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects
- **FDIC does not insure crypto assets.** https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/brochures/insured-deposits/
- ETF shares held at a brokerage are securities, but SIPC covers broker failure, not price falls. (General SIPC principle.)

**Other risks**
- **Lost keys:** Chainalysis estimated (2020) ~**3.7M BTC** is probably lost forever. https://decrypt.co/37171/lost-bitcoin-3-7-million-bitcoin-are-probably-gone-forever
- **Phishing and seed-phrase theft:** anyone who has your seed phrase owns your coins. Never type it into a website or share it with "support." (SEC bulletin and bitcoin.org above.)
- **Smart-contract bugs, bridge exploits and depegs:** UST in 2022; USDe's exchange-specific depeg on Oct 10, 2025 (see sections 1 and 2).
- **Leverage liquidations:** Oct 10, 2025 saw $19B in one day (section 1).
- **Regulatory risk:** CLARITY stalled, GENIUS rules not final, and policy can reverse more easily by agency rule than by statute.
- **Concentration of holders:**
  - Strategy (MSTR) holds **848,250 BTC** (Oct 11, 2026; average cost $75,444). https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001050446/000119312526419726/mstr-ex99_1.htm
  - US spot ETFs hold ~$100B (≈1.2M BTC, computed at ~$82K).
  - The US government holds ~328K BTC.
  - Together ≈ **12% of supply** (computed, approx.).
- **24/7 market and weekend gaps:**
  - Weekend share of BTC volume fell from 28% (2019) to ~16% (2024), a record low after the ETFs launched. Thinner liquidity means bigger moves. https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/302705/bitcoin-sees-all-time-low-weekend-trading-levels-following-etf-approvals-kaiko
  - The Oct 10, 2025 crash happened on a Friday night (UTC), while ETF markets were closed.

**Perpetual futures ("perps")**
- Perps have no expiry. A **funding payment** passes between longs and shorts to keep the perp price near spot.
  - Positive funding: longs pay shorts (crowded longs).
  - Negative funding: shorts pay longs.
- Standard design: an interest component of **0.01% per 8h** plus a premium. Because of the clamp, most prints sit at exactly 0.01%: BTC on BitMEX did so 78% of the time in Q3 2025. https://coincub.com/?p=14476
  - 0.01%/8h = **10.95%/yr** simple (computed in source).
  - Caps are much higher; Binance core contracts cap at 0.75× the maintenance margin.
- Funding is very negative in crashes (Mar 2020, Nov 2022) and high in manias (early 2021). Same source.
- Now: BTC open-interest-weighted funding ≈ **+10.1%/yr**, 30-day average +5.5%/yr (Oct 21, 2026). https://defirate.com/perp/bitcoin/snapshot/2026-10-19T0840/
- Example (computed): a $10,000 long at 0.01%/8h pays $1 per 8h, ≈ $1,095/yr.

**Liquidation math (isolated margin; computed, simplified, ignores fees and funding; each exchange's formula differs)**
- Long liquidation price ≈ Entry × (1 − 1/Leverage + MMR), where MMR = maintenance margin rate.
- Long 1 BTC at **$80,000**, **10x**, $8,000 margin, MMR 0.5%: liquidation ≈ 80,000 × 0.905 = **$72,400**. A **−9.5%** move wipes out the margin.
- Same trade at 20x: ≈ $76,400 (−4.5%). At 100x: ≈ $79,600 (−0.5%).
- Short at 10x: ≈ 80,000 × (1 + 0.10 − 0.005) = $87,600 (+9.5%).
- Compare: BTC's 43% annual volatility ≈ 2.7% daily (computed: 43%/√252). A routine day can liquidate 20x+ positions.

**What moves prices** (teach as drivers, not a formula)
- **Liquidity and rates:** BTC fell with stocks on the hawkish June 2026 FOMC, and was under pressure around the Sep 2026 hike as the 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5%. https://kucoin.com/news/flash/bitcoin-slips-after-fed-s-first-rate-hike-since-2023
  - A stronger dollar and higher real yields are a headwind: commonly cited, but the general claim is UNVERIFIED by an official study this session.
- **ETF flows:** daily US ETF net flows are reported, e.g., +$175.6M on Oct 13, 2026. https://cryptobriefing.com/?p=394338
  - Steady ETF inflows were cited as support during the July 2026 bottom. https://cryptobriefing.com/bitcoin-surges-40-percent-100k-speculation/
- **Leverage and funding:** crowded longs plus a shock produce a liquidation cascade (Oct 10, 2025).
- **Regulation and policy news:** ETF approvals, the CLARITY vote, SEC dismissals, tariff headlines.
- **Halving-cycle narrative:** past peaks came ~12–18 months after halvings. This cycle's peak was Oct 2025, ~18 months after the Apr 2024 halving (computed from dates above). It is a pattern, not a law.
- **Stablecoin supply:** a proxy for "dry powder" and on-chain liquidity. Supply is ~$301B and flat over 30 days (DefiLlama).
- **Big holders:** corporate treasuries buying or pausing. Strategy bought no BTC in some 2026 weeks and built a $3.2B USD reserve. https://www.theblock.co/post/408892/strategy-sells-263-5-million-in-mstr-shares-buys-no-bitcoin-as-usd-reserve-tops-3-2-billion

**Long-term approach and sizing**
- **Dollar-cost averaging** means investing equal amounts at regular intervals regardless of price. It "can help" manage risk over long periods; it is not a guarantee. https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/dollar-cost-averaging
- **Allocation guidance:**
  - BlackRock (Dec 2024): **1–2%** BTC is a "reasonable range" in multi-asset portfolios. https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-12-12-blackrock-recommends-bitcoin-allocation-of-up-to-2-in-multi-asset-portfolios-330590
  - Morgan Stanley GIC (Oct 2025): **0% for conservative or income portfolios, up to 4%** for "opportunistic growth"; rebalance regularly. https://www.theblock.co/post/373562/morgan-stanley-sets-4-crypto-cap-opportunistic-portfolios-aligning-blackrock-grayscale
