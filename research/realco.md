## Apple — fiscal Q3 2026 (three months ended June 27, 2026), reported July 30, 2026 after the close

Primary source: Apple 8-K Exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000320193/000032019326000018/a8-kex991q3202606272026.htm
Press release: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-reports-third-quarter-results/ (headline "Apple reports third quarter results")
Prior quarter (Q2, ended Mar 28, 2026): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000320193/000032019326000011/a8-kex991q2202603282026.htm
FY2025 Q4 (ended Sep 27, 2025): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000077/a8-kex991q4202509272025.htm

VERIFIED (company documents):
* Revenue $109,417M (+16.4% YoY from $94,036M; −1.6% vs the March quarter's $111,184M: the June quarter is seasonally smaller).
* Products $78,678M (+18.1%), Services $30,739M (+12.1%). Services = 28.1% of revenue but 42.4% of gross profit.
* Gross margin $54,770M = 50.06% (year ago 46.49%). Products GM 40.07%, Services GM 75.62%.
  Press release: gross margin "included a favorable impact of about 2 percentage points from tariff refunds"; diluted EPS
  $2.02 "included a $0.11 favorable impact from tariff refunds". (EPS without the refund ≈ $1.91, +21.7% YoY.)
* R&D $11,729M (+32.3%, 10.7% of revenue), SG&A $7,346M (+10.5%), opex $19,075M (+22.9%).
* Operating income $35,695M (+26.6%); operating margin 32.62% (year ago 29.99%).
* Other income $572M (year ago −$171M). Pretax $36,267M. Tax $6,478M (17.9% rate; year ago 16.4%).
* Net income $29,789M (+27.1%); net margin 27.2% (24.9%).
* Diluted EPS $2.02 (+28.7% from $1.57). Diluted shares 14,714.7M vs 14,948.2M (−1.6%): buybacks make EPS grow
  faster than net income.
* Categories: iPhone $54,252M (+21.7%), Mac $10,352M (+28.7%), iPad $6,191M (−5.9%), Wearables/Home/Acc $7,883M (+6.5%),
  Services $30,739M (+12.1%). Geography: Americas $45,781M, Europe $29,395M, Greater China $18,816M (+22.4%),
  Japan $6,554M, Rest of Asia Pacific $8,871M.
* Records: "our strongest June quarter ever" (Tim Cook, CEO at the time); June-quarter records for revenue, EPS, iPhone,
  Mac, Services and operating cash flow; installed base of active devices at an all-time high.
* Dividend $0.27/share, record Aug 10, payable Aug 13, 2026.
* Balance sheet Jun 27, 2026 vs Sep 27, 2025: total assets $383,266M ($359,241M); total liabilities $275,746M
  ($285,508M); shareholders' equity $107,520M ($73,733M). Retained earnings flipped from −$14,264M to +$11,326M.
  Cash + marketable securities $146,517M; debt (commercial paper + term debt) $84,344M; net cash $62,173M.
  Current ratio 1.003 (149,818 / 149,326). Inventories doubled to $11,092M (from $5,718M). Debt/equity 0.78.
  Book value per share ≈ $7.34 (equity ÷ 14,656M basic shares).
* Cash flow, nine months: operating $116,996M; capex $6,799M; free cash flow ≈ $110,197M (our math);
  buybacks $62,094M; dividends $11,778M; term-debt repaid $8,146M; commercial paper repaid $5,911M.
* Trend (revenue $M / diluted EPS): Q3 FY25 94,036 / 1.57; Q4 FY25 102,466 / 1.85; Q1 FY26 143,756 / 2.84
  (derived: six months minus Q2); Q2 FY26 111,184 / 2.01; Q3 FY26 109,417 / 2.02. TTM EPS ≈ $8.72.

CALL (July 30, 2026, 2:00 p.m. PT; speakers: Suhasini Chandramouli (IR), Tim Cook (CEO), Kevan Parekh (CFO),
John Ternus took a question). Source: call summaries (vectorshift.ai research summary; platformaeronaut AI summary;
TIKR; MacDailyNews). Paraphrase only — never reproduce transcript text. Short attributed phrases already in the
press release are fine.
* Guidance for the September quarter: revenue growth 9%–11% YoY (≈$111.7B–$113.7B on last year's $102,466M), including
  about a 2.5-point currency headwind versus June; gross margin 47%–48% including about 1 point from tariff refunds;
  opex $19.1B–$19.4B; other income ≈$350M; tax rate ≈16.5%. iPhone expected to grow in the mid-teens; Services growth
  similar to June excluding FX.
* Supply constraints on advanced chips (iPhone, Mac, iPad) expected to "increase significantly" in September.
  Memory costs rising (Cook called the memory market "a 100-year flood"); Apple raised some iPad and Mac prices.
* Q3 capital return: about $33B ($25.8B buybacks, ~$4B dividends). Cash and securities $147B, debt $84B.
* Siri AI in betas; China approval for original Apple Intelligence features.

EXPECTATIONS AND REACTION (news reports; label as "reported"):
* Consensus going in: revenue ≈ $108.7B–$109.0B; EPS ≈ $1.88–$1.89 (Shacknews, Sharecast/HL, MacDailyNews). Apple beat
  both. Services $30.74B was below a ≈$31.2B estimate (Reuters via Shacknews).
* September-quarter consensus growth ≈ 12% (≈$115B) vs guidance 9%–11% (midpoint ≈ $113B).
* Stock: closed $333.43 on Jul 30; fell ≈3% after hours; opened down ≈8.6% on Jul 31, low ≈$302; closed down ≈7.4%
  near $309 (TIKR, 9to5Mac, HL/Sharecast, MacDailyNews). P/E on TTM EPS: ≈38 before, ≈35 after.
* Analysts split: Barclays underweight $245; BofA buy $380; TD Cowen buy $400; MS overweight $360; JPM $340.
* Teaching point: a beat on the quarter, but softer guidance, a lower margin outlook and a one-time tariff boost in the
  beat → the stock fell.
* Since then: John Ternus became CEO on Sep 1, 2026 (Tim Cook stepped down). The next report (September quarter) is
  scheduled for Monday, November 2, 2026, 2:00 p.m. PT (beebom, MacTrast, MacDailyNews).
