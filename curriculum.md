# Site curriculum (October 2026)

The Stock Market Lessons site has three tracks, 46 tools and a market & tax calendar page (dates through December 2027). Figures checked October 2026 (US); limits, rates and prices change.

- **Market Basics**: What a stock is; Bonds & interest rates; Index funds & ETFs; The risk ladder; Choosing a broker
- **Reading Charts**: Candlesticks; 2-min vs 15-min; PDH, PDL, PMH, PML; Support & resistance; Breakouts & fakeouts; Volume
- **Indicators**: SMA vs EMA; 13, 48 & 200 EMA; VWAP; RSI; Volatility & expected move
- **Options & the Wheel**: Calls and puts; Contracts & moneyness; Payoff diagrams; Delta & gamma; Theta: time decay; IV & earnings; The wheel strategy
- **Risk & Sizing**: The 1% rule; Position sizing; Win rate & reward; Losses are lopsided; Which stocks to trade
- **Trading Styles**: Day, swing, wheel, value; A day-trade game plan; Value investing; Blending styles
- **Company Fundamentals**: The three statements; Earnings reports; Five numbers to check
- **Taxes for Traders**: Short vs long-term gains; Tax-loss harvesting; The wash sale rule
- **Execution & Habits**: Order types; Day trading rules in 2026; Trading psychology; Fear, greed and tilt; Keep a journal
- **Trading with AI**: What AI can and can’t do; AI as your research assistant; AI mistakes to avoid; Automate trades with Robinhood and MCP; Benefits and risks of trading bots; Setups worth automating; Backtest before you trust; Build a TradingView indicator with AI; Use AI to read news and data; Keys, privacy and the rules; AI as your trading coach
- **The Fed & Interest Rates**: How the economy moves stocks; How the Federal Reserve works; Rates, yields and the yield curve
- **Economic Data & the Calendar**: Inflation: CPI, PCE and PPI; The jobs report and jobless claims; PMI, GDP and retail sales; Trading data-release days; Earnings season and the economy; The business cycle, sectors and recessions; The investor’s calendar
- **Oil, Metals & Geopolitics**: Oil: prices, OPEC+ and the economy; Gold and silver; Copper and industrial metals; Tension, war, sanctions and tariffs; The dollar and global money
- **Crypto & Blockchain**: How a blockchain works; Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins and altcoins; Wallets, keys, exchanges and ETFs; What moves crypto prices; How to trade crypto; Risks, scams and taxes
